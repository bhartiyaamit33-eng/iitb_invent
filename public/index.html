Call for papers & posters

Give rigorous work a practical room.

PhD scholars, faculty, post-doctoral researchers and industry researchers are invited to share entrepreneurship research with an audience that includes both academic peers and practitioners. Every submission is reviewed before a presentation place is offered.

The practical details.

Dates

30–31 January 2027 at the Desai Sethi School of Entrepreneurship, IIT Bombay, Powai, Mumbai.

Registration

A ₹3,000 registration fee applies to confirmed paper presenters, poster presenters and attendees. Payment instructions follow the application decision.

Accommodation

Limited shared accommodation may be available on a first-come, first-served basis. Availability and final terms will be confirmed separately.

Publication

Publication opportunities with respected journals are being explored. Any opportunity will remain subject to the journal’s independent review process; details will be announced when confirmed.
