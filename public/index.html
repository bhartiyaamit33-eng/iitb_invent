About Inv.ent

Not a fest.
A founding day.

On 31 January 2014 the Board of Governors of IIT Bombay approved the Desai Sethi Centre for Entrepreneurship. That date is DSSE Day — the school’s foundation day. Inv.ent is the public face of that anniversary in 2027: Innovation and Entrepreneurship in one room — founders, faculty, and people who ship from IITB labs, hostels, and stubborn ideas.

DSSE trains founders in public. Since 2014: 4,300+ students through courses, ~950 mentored, ~340 venture teams, 100+ startups registered. The school sits between E-Cell, SINE, and the rest of the IIT Bombay startup stack.
